How to use this site

Four steps that beat endless reading

  1. Step 1

    Fix one document

    Rewrite the top third of your resume using achievements, not duties. That single change does more than any other hour you will spend this week.

  2. Step 2

    Write six stories

    One challenge, one failure, one conflict, one deadline, one leadership moment, one mistake. Rehearse them once out loud. This is your interview preparation.

  3. Step 3

    Verify before you engage

    Before replying to any recruiter, find their company independently and confirm the role exists. Ten minutes, every single time.

  4. Step 4

    Keep your career optional

    Current CV, warm network, visible skills and savings. Optionality is what lets you negotiate from strength instead of panic.

Before You Accept Any Job Offer

A short check that takes two minutes and protects everything else.

  • No employer, recruiter or agency will ever ask for your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV or password — not for onboarding, not for verification.
  • You are never asked to pay a registration, training, visa, security or background-check fee to be hired.
  • Nobody needs remote access to your phone or laptop. Installing an app or sharing your screen for an “assessment” is malware.
  • A real company can be verified: find it on its own website and careers page, not from the link you were sent.
  • Urgency is the warning sign. A genuine employer will not mind you taking a day to check.
