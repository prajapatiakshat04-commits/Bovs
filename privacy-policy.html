Privacy Policy

Career & Workplace Guides is an educational site with no accounts, no advertising, and no selling of data. This policy explains exactly what we collect — and the much longer list of things we never ask you for.

We will never ask for: your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, bank password, email password, or remote access to your device. Any such request claiming to be from us is a scam, and so is anything asking for it “to verify your profile” or “to process your application”.

1. Who We Are

Career & Workplace Guides publishes free, general-audience material about resumes, interviews, job-search safety, remote work and workplace communication. We are not a recruiter, agency, staffing firm, employer, law firm, financial adviser or government authority.

2. Information We Collect

  • If you send us a message: whatever you choose to write, plus your name and email if you provide them.
  • Basic technical data: our hosting provider may record standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, requested page and timestamp, for security and to diagnose faults.
  • No accounts. We operate no logins, no memberships, no user profiles and no dashboards. There is no profile for anyone to build.
What we do not do: we do not ask for or store CVs, identity documents, bank or card details, OTPs, PINs or passwords. We do not sell, rent or trade personal data. We do not send marketing email. We do not operate advertising trackers, remarketing pixels or third-party analytics.

3. Never Send Us These

  • OTPs, UPI PINs, card PINs, CVVs, net-banking or email passwords
  • Full card numbers, account numbers, or bank statements
  • Passport, driving licence, national ID or other identity documents
  • Your CV or personal documents unless we have specifically asked for a redacted copy
  • Another person's personal details, without their consent

If you send sensitive data by mistake, tell us and we will remove it. Note that for anything involving an active fraud or a job offer, we cannot verify a recruiter on your behalf — your bank's fraud line and the police are the routes that can act.

4. How We Use Information

  • To reply to your message.
  • To identify scam patterns and improve the safety guide.
  • To fix errors and add country-specific caveats where people ask for them.
  • To keep the site secure and available.

We do not use submitted information for advertising, profiling, or automated decision-making.

5. Sharing And Disclosure

We do not sell your information. We share it only where needed to operate the site or meet a legal obligation:

  • Hosting provider — processes the site's files and server logs.
  • Email provider — used to reply to your enquiry.
  • Law enforcement or regulators — if we receive a valid legal requirement, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to prevent harm.
  • Nobody else, and never for any “verification”, “shortlisting” or “processing” purpose by anyone contacting you.

We are not a law firm: this is general information, not legal advice. If your situation involves an active matter, seek qualified local advice.

6. Third-Party Services

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google's servers when a page opens. Those providers operate under their own privacy policies. We embed no advertising, no tracking pixels, no social media widgets and no analytics scripts.

7. Data Retention

  • Messages: kept only as long as needed to reply and to inform guide updates.
  • Server logs: retained briefly for security, then deleted.
  • You can ask us to delete what we hold about you at any time, subject to any legal retention we must observe.

8. Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies, no advertising cookies, and builds no behavioural profile of visitors. The only storage this site uses itself is your browser's localStorage, which remembers whether you have already dismissed the on-page alert so it does not reappear on every visit. That value stays in your browser and is never transmitted to us. If you clear your browser storage it will simply appear again.

9. Children's Privacy

This site is general information for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Please do not send sensitive data in the first place — see section 3.

11. Job Applications And Scams

We never act as an intermediary, never receive your CV on anyone's behalf, and never ask for payment. You can verify this independently: find us through a search engine or a link you trust, not one supplied in an email or message. See job search safety.

12. Changes To This Policy

We may update this policy as the site changes. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised version.

13. Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the contact page. Please describe the request clearly and do not include OTPs, PINs, passwords or full financial account numbers.

Plain-English version: no accounts, no ads, no data selling. We will never ask for your OTP or PIN. If a message asks you for those things, it is a scam — report it rather than replying.
