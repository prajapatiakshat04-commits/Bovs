Ask A Question Or Report Something Wrong

We read everything, we do not have an account system, and there is no newsletter you have to sign up for before you can write to us. Whether it is a question, a correction, or a warning about a fake recruiter, the form below reaches us.

We will never ask for: your OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, CVV, bank password, email password, or remote access to your device. Anyone contacting you and claiming to be from Career & Workplace Guides while asking for any of those is running a scam — see job search safety.

What We Can Do

  • Answer a question about anything published on this site
  • Correct an error, or add a caveat where something is country-specific
  • Add a scam pattern to the safety guide once we have confirmed it is real and common
  • Point you to the right authority or professional for your situation
  • Take a report down if you can show we have misrepresented someone or a company

What We Cannot Do

  • Get you a job, submit an application, or put you forward with any employer
  • Provide legal, HR, financial or tax advice for your specific situation
  • Recover money lost to a scam, or dispute a transaction with a bank
  • Investigate a person or a company, or verify on your behalf that an offer is real
  • Arrange interviews, or vouch for any employer
For money lost to fraud, the order matters: your bank's fraud helpline on the number printed on your card, then a police report. A message to us cannot stop a transaction. See the safety guide for the full checklist.

Reporting A Fake Recruiter Or Scam

If you want us to add a pattern to the guide, include what you can safely share:

Useful to includeNever include
The exact wording of the message, as textYour OTP, UPI PIN, card PIN, passwords
The phone number, email address or website domainFull card or account numbers, or bank statements
What they asked you to do or payYour full CV, ID documents or passport
When it happened, and whether you respondedAnother person's personal details
The company name they claimed to representScreenshots containing your own personal data unredacted

Please also report it directly where it happened — the job board listing, the platform the scam was sent through, and the cybercrime portal or local police if money is involved. That is what actually stops it reaching the next person.
