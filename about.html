About Career & Workplace Guides — Practical, Not Preachy

Career & Workplace Guides is a free, independent resource for people who would rather be told how something actually works than be sold a course. Every page answers three questions about one topic: what really happens, what good looks like, and what to do when it goes wrong.

Our Mission

To make career and workplace knowledge genuinely usable. Most advice in this space is either a fifty-page whitepaper nobody finishes, or a motivational post with no specifics. Both are useless at 9pm the night before an interview. We keep it plain, concrete and short enough to act on.

Why We Built This

Because the gap between "what works" and "what people are told" is enormous, and the cost is measured in months of unemployment, offers left on the table, and people who accept fraudulent job offers because nobody warned them what those look like. A person who knows how to verify a recruiter in two minutes is protected for their whole career. That knowledge should be free.

Who We Help

Students and fresh graduates writing a first resume. Mid-career people preparing for interviews they would rather skip. Parents and career-changers rebuilding a CV after a gap. Anyone working remotely who has noticed that things which used to happen by accident now have to be done deliberately. And anyone who has received a job offer that felt slightly too good.

What We Believe

  • Specifics beat motivation. "Cut response time from 9 hours to 2" is useful. "Believe in yourself" is not.
  • Skills are compound interest. Writing a clear email, running a good one-to-one, verifying a recruiter — all of these compound quietly over years.
  • Nobody ever owes you a job. What they owe you is a fair process. Knowing the difference is the beginning of being treated well at work.
  • Preparation reduces anxiety more than confidence does. Rehearse the story, not the pep talk.
  • Ask for what you are worth, then listen to the answer. Both halves matter.
  • Being new is not a weakness. Asking the obvious question in your first month is far better than spending a year guessing.
  • Plain language is a safety feature. If we cannot explain something simply, we do not understand it well enough yet.

How We Work

  • Educational only. General information, not legal, financial, tax or HR advice.
  • Free, with no catch. No paywall, no account, no email wall, no paid tier, no affiliate product.
  • We are not recruiters. We have no job board, no agency, no talent database, and we place nobody. We will never contact you asking for a password, an OTP, a fee or access to your device.
  • We do not sell your data. There is no advertising and no tracking of individuals.
  • No invented numbers. Salary figures vary hugely by country, role and market, so we teach you how to research yours instead of publishing a universal figure that would mislead you.
  • We update as practice changes. Hiring norms, remote expectations and scam tactics all move.

Corrections & Contributions

If something here is wrong, outdated, or specific to one country when we present it as universal, tell us. If you work in recruitment, hiring management or career coaching and can see a pattern we have missed, we would like to hear it. Reports of new scam tactics directly shape the safety guide.

One line to remember from this whole site: show evidence of results, prepare real stories, verify before you engage, and know what you are worth.
